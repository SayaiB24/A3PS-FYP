## Figure A -- operating-point trade-off

- **Checkpoint:** `notebooks/models/risk_gru_k1p0_v2_selfix_s1234.pt` (the locked seed-1234 checkpoint; see `docs/handoff/JUDGES_PREP_PLAN.md:10`).
- **Data:** `data/features/train_val_v2/*.npz` (205 clips, 103 positive, 102 negative). No feature extraction, training, or config change; `evaluate()` from `scripts/train_risk_head.py` is called unmodified.

- **Nothing re-run beyond scoring:** this is a forward pass of an already-trained checkpoint over cached features (inference only), the same class of operation `sweep_operating_point.py` already performs -- just tabulated here to include the v2/episode-based useful-warning definition, which no existing sweep `.md` file reports for this checkpoint.
- Full per-cell numbers: `figures/fig_operating_point_data.json`.

It is fed exactly the payload the dashboard gets for this frame, built by the dashboard's own `DashboardStore._slim_frames` (`scripts/dashboard_v2_data.py`) from `eval/anticipation/879/events.json`, frame_idx 544 (t = 18.133 s), over the raw frame from `data/nexar/videos/00879.mp4`. All 12 actors are `risk_level = "safe"` (the dashboard's green) and every P is 0.00 or absent at this frame.
- **Display-only departures from the dashboard:** (1) corridor outline drawn dark-navy dashed on white instead of the dashboard's faint 50 %-alpha blue (the 12 % blue fill is kept); (2) top 85 px cropped out of view to remove the Nexar watermark; (3) nine of the cars are queued within ~350 px, so the labels, which the dashboard would stack on top of each other, are laid out in three rows along the top of the frame, ordered by each car's x-position, with a thin leader line to each box. Label text and colour are unchanged.
- **Green zig-zag line under the truck (track 220, "bus"):** not a rendering artifact -- it is the dashboard's trail drawn from that track's logged centroid history, which really does jump around for this large, partly off-frame truck. Kept.
